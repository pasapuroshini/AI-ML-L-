## 📘 **Stochastic Search Methods – Notes**

### ---

### 🔹 Definition

### * **Stochastic search methods** use **randomness** to explore and identify solutions in a problem space.

* Unlike **deterministic methods**, they **introduce random behavior**, allowing the algorithm to **escape local optima**.

---

### 🔹 Key Characteristics

### * **Non-deterministic**: May yield different outcomes on different runs.

* Useful for **large**, **complex**, or **non-linear** problem spaces.

* **Efficient alternative** to exhaustive or brute-force methods.

---

### 🔹 Why Use Stochastic Methods?

### * Help in **avoiding local optima**.

* Suitable when the **search space is vast** and exact methods are computationally infeasible.

* Capable of finding **near-optimal solutions** in reasonable time.

---

### 🔹 Example: Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP)

### * Objective: Find the **shortest possible route** visiting all cities once and returning to the starting city.

* Challenge: Number of possible routes **grows exponentially** with each additional city.
